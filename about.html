A small shop with a long memory

Luna & Co. is a Manila-based online shop built around a simple idea: keep the catalogue short and choose everything carefully.

Our story

From a folding table to a full collection

In 2021, we started selling from a folding table at a weekend market in Quezon City — four bags from a maker we knew, a hand-painted sign, and a lot of patience.

Customers kept coming back for the same reason: things that were actually useful, priced without games, and backed by a person who would answer the phone.

“If we would not use it ourselves every week, it does not go in the shop.”

That rule still decides everything. It is why our collection is small, why we still pack every order by hand, and why we would rather say “we do not carry that” than sell you something we would not use.

Our mission

Fewer things, chosen better

We keep a short catalogue on purpose. It means every piece has been used, tested, and thought about before it reaches you.

How we work

Small batches, made locally where we can

Wherever possible we work with small Philippine makers and weavers, ordering in modest batches so nothing is wasted and nobody is rushed.

When a piece sells out, we restock slowly on purpose — we would rather wait for a good batch than rush a bad one into the shop.

Everything is packed and posted from Quezon City, usually within two days of your order being confirmed.
