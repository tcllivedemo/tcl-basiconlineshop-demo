How ordering works at Luna & Co.

Last updated: 2026. This is demo content for a fictional business.

Placing an order

Submitting the checkout form sends us your order request. It is not an automatic purchase. We review every order and confirm it with you by email or mobile before anything is finalised.

Payment

Payment is made manually through GCash, QR Ph, or bank transfer. We send the exact amount and account details after confirming your order. Payment is verified by our team — there is no automated verification, so please allow us time to match your transfer.

Pricing

All prices are in Philippine Peso and are as shown on the product at the time you order. Delivery fees are quoted separately and depend on your location.

Delivery

Orders are packed from Quezon City and dispatched within 2–4 days of payment being confirmed. Nationwide shipping is available; courier and timelines are agreed with you at confirmation.

Variations in handmade items

Some pieces are made in small batches or by hand. Minor differences in weave, tone, or finish are normal and are not considered defects.

Changes and cancellations

Message us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel. We can usually adjust an order that has not yet shipped.
