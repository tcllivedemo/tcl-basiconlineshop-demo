How to order

Four simple steps, no account needed. We confirm and verify every order ourselves.

  1. 1

    Choose your pieces

    Browse the collection and open any product to see its full details. Where a piece comes in options — a colour, a finish, a set size — pick the one you want before adding it to your cart.

    Set your quantity and tap Add to Cart. Your cart is kept on your device, so you can browse other pieces without losing what you have chosen.

  2. 2

    Review your cart and check out

    Open your cart to check the items, options, and quantities, then tap Proceed to Checkout.

    Fill in your name, email, and mobile number, followed by your complete delivery address. You do not need to create an account or remember a password.

  3. 3

    Choose a payment method and place your order

    Pick how you would like to pay — GCash, QR Ph, or bank transfer — then submit your order. You will receive an order reference number on the confirmation screen. Please keep it.

    Nothing is charged when you submit. No card details are ever taken on this website.

  4. 4

    We confirm, you pay, we ship

    We check your items and address, then message you the final amount — including delivery — together with the payment details for your chosen method.

    Once you have sent payment, our team verifies it manually. Payment is never confirmed automatically, so please allow us a little time to match your transfer. After that, your order is packed and dispatched.

Ways to pay

All payments are made manually and verified by our team. Details are sent to you after we confirm your order.

01

GCash

Send your payment through GCash, then message us a screenshot so we can match it to your order reference.

02

QR Ph

Scan our QR Ph code using GCash, Maya, or any QR Ph–enabled app. Works across InstaPay and PESONet.

03

Bank Transfer

Deposit or transfer to our account over InstaPay or PESONet. Send us the slip and we will confirm it manually.

This is a demonstration storefront — the payment details an actual customer would receive are the business’s own.
