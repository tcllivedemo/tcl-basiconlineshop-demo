Our story

Started at a weekend market stall

Luna & Co. began in 2021 as a single folding table at a weekend market in Quezon City, selling bags from a small local maker. It grew the slow way — one repeat customer, one recommendation, one careful order at a time.

Today we still work with the same small group of makers, and we still pack every order ourselves.
