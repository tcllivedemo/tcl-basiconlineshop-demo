Privacy Policy

A short, plain-language note on what we do with your information.

Last updated: 2026. This is demo content for a fictional business.

What we collect

When you place an order we ask for your name, email address, mobile number, and delivery address, along with the items you selected and any delivery notes you choose to add.

We also store your cart in your own browser so it is not lost if you refresh the page. That information stays on your device until you clear your browser data or complete an order.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm your order and arrange delivery.
  • To send you payment instructions for your chosen method.
  • To contact you if there is a question about your items or address.

What we do not do

  • We do not take card details and we do not process automated payments on this website.
  • We do not create customer accounts, so nothing is held in a member database.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Keeping it safe

Order details are kept for as long as needed to complete your order and handle any follow-up. If you would like your details removed, message us and we will delete them.

Questions

Email hello@lunaandco-demo.ph and we will answer personally.
